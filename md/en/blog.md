### In the Role of Guest Lecturer at the [International Psychoanalytic University of Berlin](https://www.ipu-berlin.de/en/)

This year, I had the opportunity to be invited in [Professor Sarah Bergmann's class](https://www.ipu-berlin.de/en/professoren/bergmann-sarah/), where we explored what responsible and ethical psychotherapy truly means in practice. We discussed the foundations of the therapeutic process, essential therapeutic knowledge, the differences between counseling and psychotherapy, and how ongoing learning continually enriches the therapeutic experience.

Together with the students, we worked through a clinical case focused on trauma and attachment difficulties, closely examining how traumatic experiences shape emotional regulation, relationships, and a client's inner world. We also explored the therapist's role in helping clients gradually develop emotional regulation and a sense of internal safety. An important part of the session involved inviting students to step into the therapist's role—reflecting on their emotional responses and responsibilities when encountering trauma for the first time.
