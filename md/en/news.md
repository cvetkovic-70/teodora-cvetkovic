### Join the Psychotherapy Intervision Group at the [International Psychoanalytic University of Berlin](https://www.ipu-berlin.de/en/)

Starting in October 2025, the [International Psychoanalytic University in Berlin](https://www.ipu-berlin.de/en/) hosts a psychotherapy intervision group bringing together [ALUMNI members](https://connect.ipu-berlin.de/international-alumni-program/?lang=us), practicing psychotherapists, psychotherapy trainees, and psychology students from diverse theoretical and cultural backgrounds.
The group provides a structured yet open clinical space for working with therapeutic
cases and reflecting on important questions arising in the therapeutic process. Group
work is centered on reflective dialogue across different psychoanalytic
orientations, supporting therapists in deepening clinical sensitivity and knowledge,
strengthening professional presence, and developing a more grounded
therapeutic stance within a stable and trusted group setting. <br /> <br />
If you are an [ALUMNI student](https://connect.ipu-berlin.de/international-alumni-program/?lang=us) or psychotherapist and would like to be part of a professional community dedicated to ongoing learning, reflection, and the exchange of

### Upcoming Project: Human vs. Artificial Intelligence in Clinical Diagnostics

We are pleased to present our next research initiative, which addresses one of the most pressing questions in **today’s field of psychology**:<br />
**How do human psychologists and artificial intelligence approach clinical diagnostics differently?**

### About the Project

This study will bring together psychologists from across the world — alumni of the international program at the Psychoanalytical University of Berlin — to conduct diagnostic assessments using the Personality Diagnostic Interview – PDI-5. Their evaluations will be systematically compared to analyses generated by advanced AI software, specifically designed for use in psychology.

By directly contrasting human expertise with algorithmic assessment, this project will not only highlight differences in clinical reasoning and diagnostic outcomes but also explore the potential for complementarity between human judgment and machine intelligence.

### Why It Matters

The rise of AI in healthcare raises crucial questions about the role of technology in highly sensitive fields such as mental health. Understanding where artificial intelligence aligns with, diverges from, or potentially enriches human clinical practice will provide valuable insights for the future of diagnostics, psychotherapy, and mental health care at large.
